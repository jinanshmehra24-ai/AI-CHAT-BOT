# Free Motion Graphics Tools (from GitHub)

These are for high-end motion graphics content. Everything here is free and open-source, with star counts checked in Nov 2026.
🤖 = works great with an AI assistant (Claude, etc.), which can write the animation for you.

## 1. Pick your main app

| If you use / want… | Get this | ⭐ | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **After Effects** | keep it, and add the AE tools in section 2 | — | Still the industry standard |
| **Free After Effects alternative (3D + 2D mograph)** | [Blender](https://github.com/blender/blender) | 22k | Free and pro-level: 3D, text, particles, Geometry Nodes, compositing, and a video editor |
| **Free AE-style compositing / VFX** | [Natron](https://github.com/NatronGitHub/Natron) | 5.6k | Node-based (like Nuke/Fusion): tracking, keying, rotoscoping |
| **DaVinci Resolve user** | use the built-in **Fusion** page | — | Free and node-based. Look for free Fusion macros |
| **2D vector / shape animation** | [Synfig](https://github.com/synfig/synfig) | 2.3k | Free 2D animation with bones and morphing |
| **Graphic design + animation in one app** | [Graphite](https://github.com/GraphiteEditor/Graphite) | 28k | Newer node-based 2D design app. Animation features are still growing |

## 4. Code-based motion graphics 🤖 (big opportunity)

With these you describe an animation, an AI writes the code, and it renders to MP4.
That makes them good for **kinetic typography, data/infographic videos, explainers, lower-thirds, and templated content**
where you change the text and data and re-render 100 videos.

| Tool | ⭐ | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| [Remotion](https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion) 🤖 | 71k | Videos made with React. Templated, data-driven, and endless variations. Free for individuals and small teams (check their licence for companies) |
| [Remotion templates](https://github.com/reactvideoeditor/remotion-templates) | 286 | Free ready-made Remotion effects and animations |
| [HyperFrames](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes) 🤖 | 79k | Write HTML and get a video. Built for AI agents to make animated videos |
| [Motion Canvas](https://github.com/motion-canvas/motion-canvas) 🤖 | 20k | Clean animated **explainers** with precise timing to voiceover |
| [Manim](https://github.com/3b1b/manim) / [Manim Community](https://github.com/ManimCommunity/manim) 🤖 | 96k / 42k | The 3Blue1Brown-style animation engine: maths, science, diagrams, smooth morphs |
| [Theatre.js](https://github.com/theatre-js/theatre) | 13k | Visual keyframe editor for web and 3D (three.js) animation |
| [mo.js](https://github.com/mojs/mojs) | 19k | Bursts, shapes and "pop" motion effects for the web |
| [GL Transitions](https://github.com/gl-transitions/gl-transitions) | 2.2k | 100+ free open-source **shader transitions** (glitch, zoom, morph…) |

## Suggested setup for a motion graphics creator

1. **Main work:** After Effects (or Blender if you want 100% free) + Duik + aefunctions.
2. **3D titles and logo reveals:** Blender + Geometry Nodes.
3. **Templated / faceless series content:** Remotion or HyperFrames, with an AI writing the animation code. You keep one template and render a new video in minutes.
4. **Selling more to clients:** offer Lottie exports (animated website and app icons) and 3D logo animations, not just video edits.
5. **Content idea:** post *"how I made this"* breakdowns and before/afters of your mograph. Motion designers and clients both share these.
