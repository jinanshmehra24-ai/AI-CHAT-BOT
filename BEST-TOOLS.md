# The Only Tools You Need

This is a short list for a creator who makes **motion graphics + faceless content** and does **client edits**.
Each tool earns its place by doing one of three things: **saving you editing time**, **making videos people watch to the end and share**, or **getting you clients**.
Everything is free. ✅ = already installed by this toolkit.

## Edit faster (post more = grow faster)
| Tool | Why it's in the list |
|---|---|
| **DaVinci Resolve** (free, blackmagicdesign.com) | One app for editing, colour, audio **and** motion graphics (Fusion page). Pro standard, so clients trust it |
| [auto-editor](https://github.com/WyattBlue/auto-editor) ✅ | Cuts every silence in seconds. Saves hours on client talking-head footage |
| [LosslessCut](https://github.com/mifi/lossless-cut) ✅ | Trims huge files instantly with no re-render |
| `captions.py` ✅ | Word-by-word captions. Most people watch muted, so this raises retention |

## Motion graphics (your edge)
| Tool | Why it's in the list |
|---|---|
| [Blender](https://github.com/blender/blender) | Free 3D: logo reveals, 3D titles, product shots. These stand out in feeds |
| [Duik](https://github.com/RxLaboratory/Duik) + [aefunctions](https://github.com/motiondeveloper/aefunctions) | *Only if you use After Effects:* free rigging plus ready-made bounce and inertia expressions |
| [Remotion](https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion) | Build **one animated template**, then AI changes the text and data and renders new videos in minutes. Ideal for posting daily series |

## Voice
| Tool | Why it's in the list |
|---|---|
| `voiceover.py` (edge-tts) ✅ | 400+ natural voices, zero setup |
| [Kokoro](https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro) | Best **offline** voice for normal PCs, ✅ allowed for monetised and client work |
| [Chatterbox](https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox) | *Only if you have an NVIDIA GPU:* emotional, dramatic narration and your own voice clone. ✅ commercial use |

## Sound (makes motion graphics feel premium)
| Tool | Why it's in the list |
|---|---|
| [Vital](https://github.com/mtytel/vital) | Free synth for designing your own **whooshes, risers and impacts**. They're 100% yours, with no copyright issues |
| **Pixabay SFX + YouTube Audio Library + Freesound (CC0 filter)** | Free sound effects and music that are safe to monetise |
| [Ultimate Vocal Remover](https://github.com/Anjok07/ultimatevocalremovergui) | Separates voice from music in client footage, which fixes "the music is too loud" jobs |

## Thumbnails & ideas
| Tool | Why it's in the list |
|---|---|
| [Upscayl](https://github.com/upscayl/upscayl) ✅ | Makes any image sharp enough for thumbnails |
| `ideas.py` (Ollama) ✅ | Unlimited AI ideas, scripts, hooks, titles and captions on your PC, with no Claude/ChatGPT limits |
| [OBS Studio](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio) ✅ | Records your screen for **"how I made this"** videos, which bring both followers and clients |

---

**Not in this list on purpose:** follower/like "booster" tools (bans and malware), and AI voice or SFX models with
non-commercial licences (you can't legally use them on a monetised channel or for clients).
